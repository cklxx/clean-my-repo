# Before / After Examples

## 1. Filler + hedging + verbose phrases

**Before:**

> It's worth noting that in today's rapidly evolving technological landscape,
> AI may play a significant role in transforming various industries.
> Furthermore, AI has the ability to automate a large number of tasks.

**After:**

> AI can automate many tasks, transforming industries.

## 2. AI-favorite words (Tier 1 + Tier 2 cluster)

**Before:**

> Our comprehensive platform leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver
> seamless solutions. We harness AI to navigate complexities and empower
> businesses to streamline operations.

**After:**

> Our platform uses the latest technology. We use AI to help businesses
> simplify operations.

## 3. Binary contrast + significance inflation

**Before:**

> It's not about the tools. It's about the people. This marks a pivotal moment
> in the evolution of our industry.

**After:**

> It's about the people.

## 4. False agency

**Before:**

> The decision emerges from the data. The culture shifts toward remote work.
> The market rewards companies that invest in AI.

**After:**

> The team decided based on the data. Companies shifted toward remote work.
> Buyers pay more for companies that invest in AI.

## 5. Chatbot artifacts + summary

**Before:**

> Great question! I hope this helps! In conclusion, the API supports
> pagination and filtering. Let me know if you have any questions!

**After:**

> The API supports pagination and filtering.

## 6. Preserve facts, voice, and intentional hedging

**Before:**

> PostgreSQL 16 (released Sept 14, 2023) adds logical replication on
> standby servers — a pretty big deal. The treatment may reduce symptoms,
> but the trial was small.

**After:**

> PostgreSQL 16 (released Sept 14, 2023) adds logical replication on
> standby servers — a big deal. The treatment may reduce symptoms, but the
> trial was small.

(No change to the hedging — the uncertainty is the point. "pretty big deal"
keeps the casual tone, tightened to "a big deal".)
